# Tribal Power 3.3.3

- Wireless relays are thin plates that snap onto a machine face. Seat the same item in both Bond slots to pair two plates; sneak-click a plate to extract or insert. A Water Seal rune moves fluid, an Earth Seal moves items; empty uses the plate's name. The Lattice Tuner still marks a destination face. Hoppers cannot steal Bond or Rune.
- Echo stations and Ancestral Caches have a six-face IO pad (Input, Output, Both, Closed). Sneak-use an empty hand on a face to cycle it. Hoppers and pipes honour those faces.
- Ley Lens: use to cycle ley sight, Pulse zone (8-block ring around you and nearby cores), totem voices, and Tribal machines. HUD and ground dust follow the mode. Sneak-use on a Ley Collector still prints its factor breakdown.
- Mineral grit is visible again (opaque tint and heap textures). The Ember Kiln fires every grit that has an ingot, including the shared mineral grit item.
- Spirit Codex walkthroughs for seating a Pulse Resonator and running a Lattice Conductor, with structure diagrams. Catalysts and both machines have grey how-to tooltips.
- Stone Font top no longer z-fights a copper plate at glancing angles.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
