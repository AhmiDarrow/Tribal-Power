# Tribal Power 5.6.5 - Rod and Shard

Glimmerfin come to the rod, and amethyst comes apart. Nothing new to build; saves load as they are.

- **Fish the March.** A fishing rod cast in any March water now pulls up Raw Glimmerfin in place of cod and salmon, and one fish catch in four is a Raw Silt Eel. Junk and treasure come up as they always did. Water outside the March is unchanged.
- **Catch one alive.** Hold an empty bucket in your other hand while you fish and a glimmerfin lands as a Bucket of Glimmerfin, bucket spent. Carry it home, tip it into a pond, and breed from there with Ribbon Weed or seagrass, as before.
- **Amethyst shatters.** The Echo Shatter breaks an amethyst block back into four Amethyst Shards on the usual shattering terms: Earth voice, four seconds, twenty Pulse a second.
- **Codex and hints.** The March wildlife entry covers fishing; the Raw Glimmerfin and Echo Shatter hints say so too.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
