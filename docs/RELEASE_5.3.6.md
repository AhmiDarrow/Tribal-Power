# Tribal Power 5.3.6 - Lighter drum

Half the download: Tribal Power carries only its own music again.

- **A 40 MB jar instead of 79 MB.** The Songkeeper Drum keeps Tribal Power's own 26 songs inside the mod: the Gate Rites, the Drum Circle, the music of every March land and the themes of its guardians.
- **The other songs come with their own mods.** With Ninjacat Skies Core 0.5.15 or later installed, the twelve Ninjacat guardian themes appear on the drum; with Chocobos Reborn, its eight races do. Without those mods the drum simply lists Tribal Power's songs.
- Scores and boards from 5.3.5 carry over as they are.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
