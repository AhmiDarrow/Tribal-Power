# The March, Full Breadth — biome expansion plan

Target: the March carries one more biome than the vanilla Overworld in every climate band. Minecraft 1.21.1 ships 53 Overworld biomes (the Nether's 5 and the End's 5 are out of scope). The March ships 8 today. This plan takes it to 59 across six bands, reusing the 91 species already registered and adding only blocks, trees, plants, surface rules, music and sky palettes.

Status: built on 2026-10-08 as 6.0.0 (see docs/RELEASE_6.0.0.md and RELEASE_6.0.0_INTERNAL.md). The tables below are the plan as written; the notes at the end say where the build differs.

## 3. Art and naming rules

- Blocks and items at 32 px native, five-tone hue-shifted ramps, light from the top left, tinted outline, alpha 0 or 255 (the 2026-09-19 cohesion pass in `docs/ASSET_REVIEW.md`). Woods paint in fixed colours, no biome tint. Turf and leaf blocks that should shift with climate use the biome's `grass_color` / `foliage_color` through `TribalColors`.
- Names are concrete March words, two-part compounds like the existing woods (Hearthoak, Frostpine, Bellcap). Public text never names the mods or packs a feature resembles; vanilla biome analogues appear in this internal document only.
- Every balance number that lands in Java (weather odds, spawn caps, spike damage) goes in `TribalConfig`.
- Mobs: zero new species. Every biome below draws from the 34 monsters, 50 creatures, 2 ambient and 5 water species already in the eight biome files.

## 7. Engineering

### Phase 0, foundations (do first)

1. **Biome builder script.** Replace the hand-boxed list in `rebalance_march_biomes.py` with a table-driven builder modelled on vanilla's: temperature band × humidity for inland, with continentalness (ocean / coast / inland), erosion (peaks / hills / flat) and weirdness (valley = river, high = variant) choosing the family member. Bands sit on the March's measured climate quartiles, and the `MarchSurvey` climate report calibrates them.
2. **Stretch the climate noise.** The March's temperature and humidity sit inside ±0.16 for half the map. Scale both in `noise_router` so five bands get usable width; otherwise 59 nearest-point boxes collapse into a few winners.
3. **One biome registry.** Today a biome is listed in nine places: the dimension JSON, `the_march` tag, `ModSounds.MARCH_BIOMES`, `MarchSurvey.SITES`, `PopulationCensus`, `MarchInsects`, `MarchWeather`, lang and the songkeeper charts. Add a `MarchBiomes` enum (id, band, family, music flag) that the Java sides read, and have `generate_march_worldgen.py` emit the tag and lang from the same table.
4. **Surface rules by family, not by biome.** Rewrite `generate_march_worldgen.py` so each band has one rule block with per-biome overrides, keeping the file readable at 59 biomes.
5. **Survey thresholds.** `MarchSurvey`'s spread check assumes 8 biomes. Change it to "every biome reachable within N blocks of spawn across the sampled seeds" plus a per-band share floor, and keep the Shallows wet rule.

## 9. As built (2026-10-08)

- The table lives in `tools/march_biomes.py` (ids, bands, families, placement) and `tools/march_breadth_data.py` (sky, ground, growth, creatures, remains, weather). `tools/generate_march_breadth.py` writes the biome files, the dimension list, the blocks, the tags, the names, the Codex entry and `world/MarchBiomes.java`; `tools/generate_march_worldgen.py` writes surface rules and feature lists for all fifty-nine; `tools/paint_march_breadth.py` paints the blocks and plants; the wood pipeline took the nine woods as it stood.
- Placement is a true partition of the climate space (temperature band by humidity band, continentalness, erosion, weirdness), so no biome swallows the map. Temperature and humidity are scaled by 1.6 in the noise router to give the frozen and hot bands real ground.
- Mushroom Fields has no land in the March's sea, so its analogue is the Glowcap Mire: the Reed Fen's strange, spore-lit lowland variant.
- Music is by band, not by biome: each new biome plays one of the eight existing tracks, so the jar does not grow by fifty tracks. Per-biome arrangements remain open.
- Dropped as unneeded: Snow Crust, Glacier Silt, Loam, Meadow Turf, Barrow Turf and Savanna Dust (the biome's grass tint does their job on March Grass), Rimevine and Loom Vine (the Tanglewood hangs the willow's climbable strand), Wild Hive, Glass Anemone and the Glowcap block (the Gloomcap wood's cap is the glowing block).
- Benches: the nine new woods have no Tribal Bench yet (`BenchWoods.MARCH` is a fixed list); the Codex icon repaint is `tools/paint_codex_icon.py`.
- Settlements (added the same day on the owner's note that the world looked empty): every camp in every band palette, and five jigsaw tribal villages. See RELEASE_6.0.0_INTERNAL.md, Settlements.
