# Tribal Power 5.1.0 - Your keys, your gear

Every hotkey is yours to rebind, and every piece of Spiritweave has an off switch.

## Controls

- **Tribal Power has its own heading in Options › Controls.** The four Gate Rite drums (A, S, D, F by default; the arrow keys still work), the ley goggles, the Sixfold Staff's next voice, the satchel's personal / camp vault, the Pulse HUD, and the new **Gear screen**. Everything that used to hide behind sneak-use still does; the keys are a second road to the same places. Most of them ship unbound so they cannot collide with a pack's own shortcuts: bind what you want.
- **The Gear screen** (`'` by default, the apostrophe) shows what you wear and hold: each worn Spiritweave piece with an on / off, the hood's goggles, the staff's voice, which vault your satchel opens, and the HUD.

## Spiritweave

- **Any worn piece can be switched off.** Off, it still protects like armor but gives no effect and draws no Pulse: no night sight from a hood you want dark, no speed from leggings on a narrow ledge, no slow fall when you mean to drop. The tooltip says so in red while it is off. Goggles keep their own switch.
- **The satchel names its vault.** Sneak-use still switches between your personal vault and the camp's; now a key and the Gear screen do too, and the choice is told when it changes.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
