# Tribal Power 5.3.4 - Common ground

Other mods now know what the March is like: its biomes, glass and ingots carry the common tags that packs and mods look for.

- **The March's biomes speak the common language.** Every March biome now carries the conventional `c:` biome tags for its climate and shape, so other mods' creatures, weather and features find the right homes there: the Steppe is temperate plains, the Highlands windswept mountain slopes, Glimmer Ridge a cold, snowy, magical peak, the Snow Fields cold snowy plains, the Reed Fen a wet, lush swamp, the Shallows shallow water, the Crystal Fields a rare magical dry land, and the Ember Wastes a hot, dry, dead wasteland.
- **The Reed Fen is a swamp, not plains.** It was tagged as plains, which pulled plains-only spawns into a fen. Swamp creatures from other mods now live there instead.
- **Quartz Glass works in other mods' recipes.** Every Quartz Glass block and pane now also carries the common glass tags (`c:glass_blocks`, `c:glass_panes`), which is what most mods' recipes ask for.
- **The Manifested Ingot is an ingot to other mods** (`c:ingots`).
- **Shamanic Mounts roam the March.** With Shamanic Mounts 0.1.2 or later in the pack, wild mounts live in every March biome, each with pelts suited to it.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
