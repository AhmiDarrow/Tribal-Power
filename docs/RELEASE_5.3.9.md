# Tribal Power 5.3.9 - The Spirit Anvil

Spiritgear can be mended at last, and totems bind again.

- **The Spirit Anvil.** Anvil-iron horns around a Block of Manifested Metal, on a Resonant Core, standing on three Manifested Ingots. Craft it from an iron block, a Block of Manifested Metal and an iron block across the top, a Resonant Core in the middle and three Manifested Ingots across the bottom.
- **Mend Spiritgear with Manifested Ingots.** Put a worn Spiritgear tool or weapon on the left and Manifested Ingots on the right: each ingot mends a quarter of its durability for a level, and only the ingots the mend needs are taken.
- **Still an anvil.** It renames, adds enchanted books, combines tools and mends other gear with its own material, just like an iron anvil, and it falls like one. Unlike an iron anvil it never chips or breaks.
- **Block of Manifested Metal.** Nine Manifested Ingots make a block and it crafts back into nine. It counts as a beacon base and as a storage block for other mods.
- **Sneak-click binding works again.** Sneak-clicking a Resonance Totem while holding Spiritgear or a Spirit Charm gives it the totem's voice once more (it costs 40 Pulse from a carried cell). The game had been handing the click to the held item and skipping the totem.
- A Codex page under Gear shows the Spirit Anvil.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
