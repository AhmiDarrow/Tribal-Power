# Tribal Power 3.5.0 - The March reborn

- **The March runs smoothly.** Plants used to be scattered by the thousand in every chunk, and some of them glowed and shed particles. Flora now grows in groves and drifts, and every biome is held to a plant and light budget. Ley Thistle no longer glows, and Echo Blooms shed far fewer motes.
- **New terrain.** Terraced mesas in the crystal country. Each biome has its own ground: basalt and rubble in the Ember Wastes, mud channels in the Reed Fen, snow and packed ice in the Snow Fields, calcite in the Crystal Fields, bare slate on steep slopes. Four tree shapes and bushes grow in groves. There are boulders and crystal clusters.
- **Every March block and plant is repainted.** Layered slate, crumbly soil, tussock grass, ridged farmland, trodden paths, cushion moss, grained planks. Grass, leaves and March Leaf take each biome's own colour.
- **Underground.** Much bigger cave systems, plus canyons. All the vanilla ores now generate in the March, set in March slate: March Coal, Iron, Copper, Gold, Redstone, Lapis Lazuli, Diamond and Emerald Ore. They mine, drop and smelt like vanilla and carry the usual ore tags. Emeralds are in the Highlands only. Small amethyst pockets grow on cave walls.
- **Game from the March's creatures.** Dawn Stags drop Raw Game and March Leather. Mossbacks and March Walkers drop Raw Game. Roast it in a furnace, smoker or campfire. March Leather crafts into vanilla leather and is tagged `c:leathers`. Game counts as meat for wolves and for other mods.
- **Gate Drum return always brings you home.** Striking a drum in the March takes you back to where you left, or to your spawn if that spot is gone. It no longer fails beside a campfire or on a slab, and it no longer builds a stone pad in your base. Your return point now survives death.
- **With Chocobos Reborn installed** (optional; Tribal Power never needs it): chocobos roam all six March biomes. Flame birds hatch in the Ember Wastes, and Snow Fields birds on snow or ice can roll Wonderful. Farms stand in the Steppe and Highlands, and March Leaf sheds Gysahl seeds. With Chocobos Reborn 1.0.4 or newer, Wild Gysahl also grows in the Reed Fen and on the open turf.
- **Seal Loom** faces whoever places it. Its model warning in the log is gone.

**Existing worlds:** the first time a save loads 3.5.0, its old March is moved to `tribalpower_backups` inside the save and the dimension regenerates. This happens once. Anything built in the March moves with it to the backup; nothing is deleted. Players who logged out in the March wake on new ground. To keep an old March exactly as it is, set `retrogenMarch = false` in `tribalpower-common.toml` before loading. The Overworld is untouched.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
