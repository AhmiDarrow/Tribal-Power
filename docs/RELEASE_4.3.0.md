# Tribal Power 4.3.0 - The March breathes

The March now acts on its own: weather of its own making, ley surges, festival days and wandering spirits, and for the first time it has music.

## A living March

- **March weather.** Four countries have weather beyond rain: **Ashfall** over the Ember Wastes, a **Glimmer Storm** over the Crystal Fields, a **Whiteout** on the Snow Fields and **Fen Mist** in the Reed Fen. Each closes the world in with its own fog and particles and a sound of its own, spirits rise under it by day as they would at night, and each favours one voice's generators while it hampers another's: Ashfall feeds Ember Horns and chokes Wind Harps, a Glimmer Storm feeds Wake Bells and dulls Drumhearts, a Whiteout drives Wind Harps and freezes Wave Drums, Fen Mist swells Wave Drums and damps Ember Horns. Weather comes and goes on its own, whether or not anyone is there to see it.
- **Ley surges.** Now and then one voice's ley threads run bright. Through the Ley Lens they blaze and the lens names the voice; every Ley Collector whose threads carry it yields double while the surge lasts. Stand on a bare crossing, the point where a cell's threads meet, with no collector to drink it, and the surge gives you **Ley Sickness**: the ground sways and your Pulse drains.
- **Festivals.** Every tribe keeps a festival day, the nine taking turns through a cycle. On their day the camp's fires burn, the hearth throws its colour into the air and the Kin keep to the hearth. The Elder has festival words and a gift for anyone who joins the feast; a rite performed in the camp that day earns festival thanks, and a feast eaten by the hearth counts you at their table.
- **Wandering spirits.** Rarely a warm light drifts near you out in the March. It does not flee: reach it and it tells you a line of its country's story and leaves a small gift, then goes.
- **The Codex** has a new chapter, *The living March*, whose first page shows what the March is doing right now: the weather, the surge, whose festival it is.

## Music and soundscape

- **Eight biome tracks**, one for every March country, in the pack's own instruments: skin drums, horn drones, flutes and chant, with the wind, the water, the crackle or the bells of the place under them.
- **Boss themes** for The Unsung, the Weeping Colossus and each of the eight guardians, playing while the fight is on and fading when it ends, and a theme for the Ninth Agreement.
- **Stingers** when a festival begins, a surge starts or the weather turns.
- **Guardian voices.** Each guardian has a voice of its own now, for its calls and its fall.
