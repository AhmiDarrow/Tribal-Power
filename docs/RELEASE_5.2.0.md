# Tribal Power 5.2.0 - Deeper cells

Pulse Cells hold six times what they did, and a third size sits above the Greater.

- **A Pulse Cell holds 1,200** (it held 200), and a **Greater Pulse Cell 4,800** (it held 1,200). Cells already in your world keep their charge and simply have more room. Nothing about filling them changes: a Drumheart, a Resonator or a generator tops them up as before.
- **The Grand Pulse Cell holds 19,200.** A Greater cell ringed by four Resonant Cores and four Manifested Ingots. Right-click it onto a piece of Spiritgear or Spiritweave holding a smaller cell to upgrade it, the way a Greater upgrades a plain one; the old cell comes back to your cursor.
- The Spirit Codex's Spirit Pulse, Spiritgear and Spiritweave pages say so.

Ranked gear, anointed weapons, songs and long trips through the March have been spending faster than a pocketful of cells could feed; this is the other side of that.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
