# Tribal Power 5.3.2 - Bound stones

A fix for servers and single-player worlds running AgriCraft 4.0.17: 5.3.1 would not start with it.

- **Worlds start again with AgriCraft 4.0.17.** The March crops that 5.3.0 and 5.3.1 added for newer AgriCraft left out seed fields that 4.0.17 needs, and it stopped the world from loading ("Failed to load registries"). The seeds now list their drop chances and the crops list the seasons they grow in (all four), and Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean and Steppe Grain load as AgriCraft crops.
- Everything else is as in 5.3.1: touching Pulse Cairns join into one rune-carved stone that glows with its charge, piles of up to 64 stones and 256,000 Pulse, Pulse Cells that fill in one use, and the see-through gaps closed on the Ley Collector, Stone Font, Ancestral Cache and the rest.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
