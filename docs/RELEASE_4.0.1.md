# Tribal Power 4.0.1 - One hearth

The rest of the camp wears the same stone, wood and copper as the 4.0 workshops, and a camp that is standing still does less work. Pulse numbers are unchanged.

## The hearth, finished

- **The workshop row has its own shapes.** Echo Unweave is a copper ring with a violet crystal and still faces you. The Song Bench stays a table. The Ley Collector is a bowl, the Pulse Resonator a ring of posts around a crystal that burns ember while it runs, the Lattice Conductor a slim mast, the Lattice Converter a hooped barrel, and the Harmonic Energizer a copper body with wood cheeks. The Drumheart keeps its drumhead. The Seal Loom keeps its threads and its disc. The Resonance Mesh is a grid, the Stone Font a basin, the Ritual Brazier a bowl of embers, and the Rite Pedestal a narrow post so it is not a second brazier. The Spirit Cistern is a banded tank. The Ancestral Cache is a wood chest and the Deep Cache is stone with a crystal seal.
- **The rest of the placeables took the hearth materials.** Drums, pumps, harps, lanterns, furniture, plates, relays, kinship totems and tribe banners keep their shapes and their crests. The flat stone, plain planks and orange copper on them are now the coursed deepslate, seamed wood and worn copper. Tribal Benches still use each wood's own planks. Only the copper trim moved.

## Quieter while it stands

- **Ley lines skip veins that cannot reach you.** A thread with no totem of its colour bending it is left alone when even its widest sway misses. Once a thread is already within half a block, the search stops. Wearing the goggles, the bends rebuild about twice a second instead of every frame. The threads look the same, and a collector still counts the same lines.
- **A full generator no longer dirties its chunk every second.** The beat still runs, and fuel is still spent only for Pulse that was actually stored.
- **A Lattice Conductor marks its chunk only when a saved number moves.** An idle conductor keeps its beat without rewriting the chunk every second.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
