# Tribal Power 5.4.0 - The March Remade

Every creature in the mod has a new body, the Spirit Codex is now a full bestiary drawn from those models, and relay plates learn channels.

- **Every creature remade.** All 97 creatures have new models and skins, each shaped like the animal its name promises: the Lantern Fox's look-alikes are now a jerboa, a lynx, a star-nosed mole, a rift-torn sighthound, an eyeless cave ghoul and a saddled tapir. The moths each have their own shape, the guardians are rebuilt from the ground up, and the Stampede Spirit's rump, the Prism Serpent's tail and the Silt Eel's head are whole again. Overlapping faces that flickered at a distance are gone.
- **A true bestiary.** The Codex draws each creature from its own live model, turning slowly; drag it to turn it yourself. Every creature has its own page, with where it lives, what it drops and what it eats.
- **The Codex, checked against the game.** Around two hundred pages were corrected or filled in, from group sizes and drop counts to how plates, totems and rites really behave, and new pages cover what was never written down.
- **Relay channels.** A ranked relay plate opens more channels: two at Attuned, three at Bound, four at Manifested. Each channel has its own destination and filter, and the plate sends to them in priority order or in turn.
- **Void plates.** A Fire Seal in a plate's Rune slot burns what the plate pulls, but only what its filter allows. An empty filter burns nothing.
- **Plates go on with a plain click.** Right-click a relay plate onto a machine without crouching. The Lattice Tuner marks caches and chests and aims plates with a plain click as well, and the Stone Font takes item and fluid plates on its top and bottom.
- **Two-block totems.** Resonance Totems stand two blocks tall, and either half can be clicked. Old totems grow their top back. Ritual Chalk links totems with a plain click again.
- **Gathering Charm.** A new Air-voiced charm draws nearby items and experience to you.
- **The Earth axe fells whole trees.** A Spiritgear axe carrying Earth brings a whole tree down from the top, wherever you cut it.
- **Familiars at the heart of magic.** Attune a bonded familiar at a Resonance Totem. Its voice makes your charms and Spiritgear of that voice cheaper, your songs stronger and your rites longer, and it keeps nearby totems of its voice awake.
- **Game, leather and herds.** Gentle creatures drop Raw Game and March Leather, and every one of them can be bred with its own food.
- **More Glimmer Ridge.** The Glimmer Ridge is about half again as common, and its grazers can now rise on its moonstone and moss agate.
- Camp faces set to None offer no slots at all.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
