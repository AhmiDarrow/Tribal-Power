# Tribal Power 5.3.11 - Crops That Grow

The March crops grow where you plant them, and there are more of them to find.

- **They grow on crop sticks now.** On AgriCraft the five March crops asked for soil almost nothing is: Emberroot and Steppe Grain wanted dry ground, Fen Rice wanted standing water, and a fresh seed had no tolerance at all, so a crop planted on farmland, or even on most March soil, sat at its first stage forever. Each crop now grows on farmland and on the March's own grass, soil and moss from strength one, and a stronger seed still stretches further.
- **Three times as many wild patches.** Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean and Steppe Grain now grow wild in every chunk of their own March country instead of one chunk in three.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
