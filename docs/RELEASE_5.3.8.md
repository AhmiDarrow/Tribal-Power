# Tribal Power 5.3.8 - The Spirit Anvil

Spiritgear can be mended at last.

- **The Spirit Anvil.** Anvil-iron horns banded with Manifested metal, on a Resonant Core, standing on three Manifested Ingots: craft it from two iron blocks and a Manifested Ingot across the top, a Resonant Core in the middle and three Manifested Ingots across the bottom.
- **Mend Spiritgear with Manifested Ingots.** Put a worn Spiritgear tool or weapon on the left and Manifested Ingots on the right: each ingot mends a quarter of its durability for a level, and only the ingots the mend needs are taken.
- **Still an anvil.** It renames, adds enchanted books, combines tools and mends other gear with its own material, just like an iron anvil, and it falls like one. Unlike an iron anvil it never chips or breaks.
- A Codex page under Gear shows how it works.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
