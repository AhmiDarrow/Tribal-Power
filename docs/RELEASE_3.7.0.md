# Tribal Power 3.7.0 - The carried kit

- **Spiritgear Shears.** The set gains shears, and they rank and bind like the rest of it. Earth takes the whole 3x3 face of leaves, wool or vine in one swing. Air trims for nothing at all: no Pulse, no wear. Water leaves a sheep its fleece, always at Manifested. Fire, used on a beehive, smokes the bees calm so none swarm you. Spirit mends what it shears and shows which beasts are worth shearing. Loom sends the cuttings straight to hand.
- **Spiritgear Hoe.** Use it on a ripe crop and it reaps and sows in one motion: the harvest drops, one seed goes back into the ground, and the plant is left standing. Earth turns a 3x3 of soil at a time. Water leaves its furrows wet and keeps the fields you walk past watered. Air reaps a 3x3; Loom reaps a 5x5 and spends no Pulse doing it. Spirit sends a quarter of harvests back doubled.
- **Spirit Flask.** A tank you can carry: 4,000 mB in one hand, 16,000 in the **Greater Spirit Flask**, and never a stack of half-empty buckets. Use it on a cistern, a Wave Drum, a relay or another mod's tank and it draws from it; if it cannot draw, it pours in what it carries. Use it on a fluid source in the world and it drinks a bucket; sneak-use and it pours one back out. Other mods see an ordinary fluid container, so pipes and filling machines fill it where it sits.
- **Totem Wrench.** Machines end up facing the wrong way. The wrench turns a block instead of breaking and replacing it, and sneak-used on a machine face it steps that face through Both, Input, Output and Closed — the setting the machine's own side pad shows, now reachable from outside the screen. A turn costs 1 Pulse from any cell you carry, or a point of the wrench's 512.
- **Weaver's Wand.** Click the face of a block and the wand carries that face outward across every neighbour like it, up to 32 blocks in one sweep. The blocks come from your pack at 2 Pulse each. It only fills open air, so a floor goes down in a click and nothing underneath is lost.
- **A tidy button on every inventory.** Your own pack, Ancestral and Deep Caches, and any chest, barrel, shulker box or hopper now carry a small **Tidy** button in the corner above their slots. A press merges what can be merged and lays the rest out in order. Your hotbar and armour are never moved, and a machine's slots are left alone — the button only appears where every slot takes anything. Set `sortButtons = false` in `tribalpower-client.toml` to hide it.
- **Lighter on the server, and on frames.** A pass over the mod's busiest code. Machines, benches and the Lattice Conductor find nearby totems, caches and benches by looking at what is actually there instead of testing every block in a cube around them, which is thousands of lookups a second off a working camp. Spirit sight for ores and buried chests skips whole slabs of stone that cannot hold what it is looking for. Reading a piece's rank and voice no longer copies its data every time, which it was doing several times a tick for every worn piece and again for every frame it was drawn. The Echo station screen looks its recipe up when the input changes rather than twice a frame, the Pulse readout and the Ley Lens read once a tick instead of once a frame, charms do one pass over what you are wearing and nothing at all when you wear none, frost boots look where you walk every few ticks rather than every one, totems remember the land instead of re-reading it each tick, blessed ground grows on a slower beat for the same growth, and effects that nobody is near are no longer sent.

- **Fitting in.** The new Spiritgear takes ranks at the Echo stations, unweaves back into a Manifested Ingot, carries a seated Pulse Cell, accepts Pulse Attunement, and glows in its bound voice like the rest of the set. All five additions have Codex pages and hand-drawn art at every rank.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
