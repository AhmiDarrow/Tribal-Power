# Tribal Power 3.4.3

- Spiritgear is repainted. The pickaxe, axe, shovel and blade are shaded 32px sprites, and each rank wears its own fittings: thread, Attuned copper, Bound light, Manifested gold.
- The Spiritgear Blade is a slightly curved single-edged long sword with four runes on the flat. The runes take the rank's inlay.
- The Spiritgear Axe faces forward in the hand. The pickaxe head is slimmer and even on both sides of the haft.
- Spiritweave icons are redrawn: a real hood, a belted robe, leggings with knee wraps, bound boots.
- Worn Spiritweave is painted for the body. Open-faced hood with a brow brim, robe with collar, belt, hem and back sigil, cuffed sleeves, leather-soled boots, leggings with a sash and side seam. It used to show one tiled pattern on every face.
- Resonance Maul, Sixfold Staff, Wayfarer Satchel and the Spiritweave bolt are repainted to match. The staff ring shows the six voices.
- Art only. No recipes, stats or saves change.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
