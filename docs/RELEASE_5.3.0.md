# Tribal Power 5.3.0 - Piled stones

Pulse Cairns pile up into one store, a Pulse Cell fills in one go, and the March crops finally reach AgriCraft.

- **Touching cairns are one pile.** Set Pulse Cairns against each other on any side and they hold and spend as one: two stones are an 8,000 store, three 12,000, up to 64 stones and **256,000 Pulse**. Any stone reads the whole pile, fills and drains it, and gives a comparator its level. Each stone still holds its own 4,000 inside, so a stone you break takes only its share and the rest keep theirs. The old five-stone column limit is gone. A bigger pile also fills faster: 200 a second for every stone in it.
- **A Pulse Cell fills as far as the source holds.** One right-click on a Drumheart, a Resonator, a generator or a cairn pile tops the cell up as far as it has room, rather than 25 or 100 at a time.
- **The March crops reach AgriCraft.** AgriCraft 4.0.17 reads its crops, soils and crosses from different folders than the build this mod was made against, so in a pack with the CurseForge release none of Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean or Steppe Grain, and none of the March soils, were ever known to it. They ship in both places now and load on either.
- The Spirit Codex's Pulse Cairn and Spirit Pulse pages say so.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
