# Tribal Power 5.3.17 - Flat Glass

Quartz glass lies down: floor and ceiling sheets in every colour, lit or not.

- **Horizontal quartz glass panes.** Any quartz glass pane, clear, dyed, or lit, crafts on its own into a flat sheet, and the sheet crafts back into a standing pane. Place it like a slab: on the top of a block or the lower half of a side for a floor sheet, under a block or the upper half for a ceiling sheet.
- **Sheets join up.** Sheets side by side at the same height share their edges and read as one surface. Open edges show a rim. Dyed sheets tint a beacon beam the way stained panes do.
- **Lit sheets.** A lit pane makes a lit sheet that shines at full brightness, and an unlit sheet takes the glowstone in an Echo Attune like any other quartz glass.
- **Lit glass is clearer.** Lit quartz glass blocks and panes are less cloudy, closer to clear glass.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
