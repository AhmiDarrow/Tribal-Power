# Tribal Power 4.1.0 - The spirit layer and the table

The March learned to hurt you in new ways, and the camp learned to mend you. This release adds the spirit layer -- blessings, boons and afflictions -- and everything a tribe cooks, along with a new weapon family, shamanic healing, and a long list of fixes from a full sweep of the mod.

## The spirit layer

- **Six voice blessings**, one per Attunement, shown in the voice's colour. Earth digs faster and stands firm; Fire's blows burn, and at level II fire cannot touch you; Water swims faster and mends while wet; Air falls softly and jumps higher; Spirit shows hostiles through walls; Loom threads a tenth of every Pulse spend back. A Ward song grants its voice's blessing, a seal rite at a Rite Pedestal blesses everyone in the circle, and a feast shares one round the table. A worn Lantern charm keeps them lit half again as long.
- **Nine tribe boons**, one per tribe, carried always at Kin standing and for a while by anyone who eats that tribe's dish. The Pad-keepers never trample a field; the Grit-singers break more from ore; the Rootbinders hurry the crops around them; the Edge-walkers never fall while they sneak; the Drumhearts swing faster; the Pattern-weavers make Echo stations near them work double; the Colony-keepers are never stung; the Seal-carvers' charms ask half the Pulse; the Loom-stitchers sing cheaper.
- **Afflictions.** The Unsung's Silence bolts leave an **Unsung Hush**: no song or staff voice answers until it lifts. An Echo Weaver's touch leaves you **Frayed**, torn of two hearts, and milk does nothing for it. **Ley Sickness** is registered for the surges to come. Every remedy, the Healing Circle and a night in the sweat lodge lift all of them, and so does washing in a **Spirit Well**: sneak in its water with Pulse in a cell.

## The table

- **Five March crops** grow wild in their own country and on your farmland: Emberroot, Fen Rice, Frostberry, Glimmer Bean and Steppe Grain. Each is its own seed. The Spiritgear Hoe reaps and replants them; bees pollinate them; they carry the common crop and seed tags.
- **The Hearth Pot** cooks over a campfire, fire, magma, lava or an Ember Bowl and asks no Pulse: up to four ingredients and a bowl, in any order. Its meals are data-driven, so a pack can add its own, and JEI lists them under the pot.
- **Nine tribe dishes**, one per tribe, each carrying its tribe's boon for five minutes. Kin sell theirs once they call you Friend, and a tribe prizes its own dish above any gift but a reagent.
- **Six feasts**, one per voice: set one down and it serves six, and every serving blesses the eater with that voice.

## Shamanic healing

- **The Spirit Kettle** brews a raw reagent, a March herb and a base into remedies: bottles make tinctures to drink, honeycomb makes salves to lay on yourself or a friend, charcoal makes incense to burn in a Ritual Brazier for everyone around. The reagent's family picks the remedy; the nearest totem's voice shapes it.
- **Spirit Sickness.** March elites and spirits that rose at night can leave it on you; it stacks, costs health and speed, and only shamanic remedies lift it. **Spirit Blessing** keeps it from taking hold.
- **The Healer's Rattle** is Spiritgear that mends whoever you look at, for a little Pulse, and ranks like the rest.
- **The Healing Circle** is a new Spirit rite: it heals, cleanses and blesses everyone in the circle, stays open ten minutes, and calls back any fallen familiar whose **Spirit Remnant** you laid on its pedestals. A bonded familiar that dies now leaves one behind.
- **Sweat Stones** steam over heat. Sleep the night near them under a roof and you wake whole and blessed.

## Weapons

- **Eight Spiritgear weapons** join the Blade: spear, halberd, battle axe, warhammer, dagger, scythe, greatsword and trident. Each ranks at the Echo stations, takes a totem voice and has one move of its own; each trades reach, speed, weight and damage against the others, and every one has a bone handle. Long weapons are drawn at their real length in your hand.
- **Anointing.** The Song Bench has a weapon seat: four empowered reagents work the reagent's power into the weapon, one at a time, until another replaces it. Eleven anointments, from Bloodthirst's life steal to Stormcall's lightning, on Spiritgear, swords, axes, tridents and maces alike.
- **The Pulse Crossbow** loads slower than the bow but hits harder, flies truer and holds its shot.

## The camp kit

- **The Spirit Door** is now a threshold: you, your camp, your familiars and every gentle creature walk through; monsters and hostile spirits meet a wall.
- **Vine Lifts** carry you up a column with a jump and down with a sneak, one lift per floor, no linking.
- **The Songkeeper Drum** is the Gate Rite as a game: every track of the pack, your best scores, and a board of the server's five best.
- **Soul Urns** in woven, copper, manifested and resonant tiers carry a creature home. Never a player, a boss, an elite or another player's companion.
- **Reagent dyes**: grind a reagent with chalk in a bowl for two dye; sixteen reagents make the sixteen colours.
- **Tribal Power doors open in pairs**, and the Tribal Bench keeps its grid and takes JEI recipes with a click.

## The March

- **Day is safer and night is the danger.** Spirits rarely rise in daylight and fade at dawn under open sky; at night they rise stronger and some rise as elites, in pairs at most, and stop once four are near. Sleeping in the March moves the clock to morning, and only a spirit already hunting you keeps you from bed. Every number is in the config.
- The Spirit Codex remembers when you turn spoilers on, per player, and every new system has its pages, written the way the rest of the book is.

## Fixed

- Machines with open models no longer show the sky through their neighbours.
- A Resonator's catalyst keeps its wear when lifted; a Resonance Mesh can no longer stall on a cycle dearer than its buffer; a fast redstone clock earns no more from a Drumheart than a steady one; a machine far from spawn keeps its side settings.
- The Tribal Bench no longer hands two players one result, and drops its shelf when broken. Polished Moss Agate drops again. The Weaver's Wand cannot make two slabs from one. The last verse arrow keeps its verse. The Spirit Flask leaves kelp alone. Song sheets can be sung and bound.
- Far Gate links can be made in survival, and a Far pair rebuilt as Way frames no longer crosses worlds. Chalk links stay in one world.
- Only gentle creatures and the six voiced remnants can be bonded; new creatures want their own reagent, not seagrass; water dwellers spawn in water; a boss's retinue leaves with it; sleeping through a March night ends the rain.
- The Song Bench and Pouch buttons act on the reagent you click; the Codex, drum, bench and cache screens fit small windows; the Sweat Lodge cannot be farmed by lying down and getting up; spirit effects survive a relog.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
