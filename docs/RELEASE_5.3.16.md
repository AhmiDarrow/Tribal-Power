# Tribal Power 5.3.16 - Stone and Lit Glass

The March's stones craft the rest of a building set, and quartz glass can finally be lit.

- **Pressure plates and buttons** for March stone, March cobble, moonstone, and moss agate, including the polished and brick forms. Two of that stone in a row make a pressure plate that players and mobs can trigger. One stone makes a button that stays down for a second.
- **Chiseled bricks** for moonstone and moss agate. Stack two of that stone's brick slabs, or cut them on the stonecutter. March stone already had chiseled bricks.
- **Cracked bricks.** Smelt March stone bricks, moonstone bricks, or moss agate bricks. The stonecutter cuts them as well.
- **Lit quartz glass.** Echo Attune takes glowstone dust in a catalyst slot. Seat quartz glass, a block or a pane, with a Fire totem nearby, and the light is bound into it. The colour stays. Lit glass can be dyed the same way as clear glass, and six lit blocks cut into lit panes.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
