# Tribal Power 5.6.3 - Said Once

A fix and a polish pass. Nothing new to build; saves load as they are.

- **Hints print once.** With Ninjacat Skies Core, Chocobos Reborn or Shamanic Mounts installed, every hint line showed once per mod, four times over. Each mod now speaks only for its own items.
- **Picked-up reagents behave.** A stack the Reagent Pouch absorbs whole now flies to you with the pickup sound and counts toward the stat, instead of vanishing silently.
- **Machines remember.** The Resonance Mesh's band, the Stone Font's ask, the Echo Station's recipe and an emptied Pulse Resonator are saved the moment they change; a chunk unloading right after a switch no longer brings back the old setting.
- **Lighter beats.** The Seal Loom tests only its own recipe each beat instead of every recipe in the pack; the Ley Lens reads what the collector's tick already measured instead of sampling the field again; pouch, chalk, cell and compass tooltips and HUD bars stop copying their data every frame; the Ley sight HUD is translatable.
- **Tidy-ups.** Drum duels nobody finished no longer linger for the server's life; a player's placing snapshot clears on logout; a bad codex image name no longer breaks a page; a hopper on an odd face of a Rite Pedestal or Ancestral Cache no longer crashes; Tribal Power's chocobo hook no longer touches every entity that joins the world.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
