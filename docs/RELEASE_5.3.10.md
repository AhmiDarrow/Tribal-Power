# Tribal Power 5.3.10 - Three Moons over the March

The March sky gets a sun and moons worthy of its painted heavens.

- **A new sun.** A high-definition sun with a soft corona replaces the old pixel sun over the March.
- **Three moons.** The Pale, a great silver moon with grey seas and bright craters, keeps the old moon's place and phases, so everything that waits for a full moon still does. The Loom, a jade moon threaded with glowing teal veins, drifts against the sun over nine days. The Ember, a small rust moon with glowing cracks, swings round every four.
- **Real phases and paths.** Each moon crosses the sky on its own tilted path and is lit from the side the sun is on, from new to full. The glowing veins and cracks still show on their dark sides; by day the moons fade like a daytime moon, and a moon that crosses the sun eclipses it.
- **A lighter sky.** The March's painted sky is now built once on the graphics card instead of every frame, so it costs less to draw than before, moons and all.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
