# Tribal Power 5.3.5 - The Songkeeper's Drum

The Songkeeper Drum becomes a real rhythm game: 46 whole songs, four difficulties, and drum duels between friends.

- **46 songs on the Songkeeper Drum.** The Gate Rites and the Drum Circle, the music of every March land, the themes of its guardians, the Ninjacat Skies guardian themes and the Chocobos Reborn races, every one played whole. Each chart is made from the recording itself, so every note lands on a beat you can hear.
- **Four difficulties.** Easy (three drums, and it never breaks), Normal, Hard and Expert, with your best score and stars kept for each, and a board of the five best on the server.
- **A proper drum game.** Notes ride a highway toward four drum heads. Perfect, Great and Good hits, a streak multiplier up to x4, glowing Spirit Surge phrases that charge a double-points burst on Space, a Resonance meter that breaks the song if it empties, streak callouts, and a results screen with stars and full combos. A missed note makes the song dip.
- **Drum duels.** Set two Songkeeper Drums side by side and challenge whoever stands at the other one. You both play the same song at once, each watching the other's score in a tug-of-war bar, and the higher score wins. Your duel record is kept.
- **Settings that keep the notes on the music.** An audio offset to match your machine, note speed, and optional hit sounds, all in the song list.
- **The Gate Drum's rite on the new game.** The way into the March plays exactly as before, its own drum beat and 60% to open, now on the new highway with the new hits and a meter showing the mark you need.
- **The green recipe book at the Tribal Bench.** It opens beside the bench and lays recipes out on the grid, just like a crafting table.
- **A tidier Reagent Pouch.** Reagent icons, raw and empowered side by side, only what you hold unless you ask for all, framed inventory slots, and a Take button that says how many. Shift-click or double-click a reagent to take it at once.
- **Wayfarer Satchels stay out of their own vault.** A satchel can no longer be put into the vault it opens. A Reagent Pouch keeps its reagents in the vault, chests and shulker boxes alike.
- **New sapling art.** Hearthoak, Frostpine, Bellcap, Cinder, Strider and Willow saplings are repainted to match their trees. The March sapling is unchanged.
- **Spirit Charms cost what they are worth.** Every charm is now a full recipe of its seal, Manifested Ingots and spiritweave with fitting rarities. The Sky Charm, which grants flight, now takes a Nether Star.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
