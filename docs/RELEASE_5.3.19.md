# Tribal Power 5.3.19 - Filtered Relays

Relay plates get a filter, and their hitbox finally sits where the plate is.

- **A filter on every plate.** Open a relay plate to find a Rune slot and eight filter slots. Click a filter slot with an item to mark it, with an empty hand to clear it, or shift-click goods in your inventory. The slots hold a picture of the item, never the item itself. For fluid, mark a bucket or any filled container.
- **Whitelist or blacklist.** The button beside the filter switches it. A blacklist holds the marked goods back and lets everything else cross; a whitelist sends only the marked goods. Plates start as an empty blacklist, so they move everything just as before. Crouch and right-click a plate to switch it without opening it.
- **Aim plates with the Lattice Tuner.** The Bond slot is gone. Mark the face that should receive the goods with the tuner, then use the tuner on the plate.
- **Bonded pairs carry on.** A pair from an older world turns into the same tuner link the first time both plates are loaded, and both bond items pop out beside the plates. Opening an old bonded plate hands its bond back.
- **The hitbox fits the plate.** A plate on the side of a machine used to have its hitbox on the far side of its block, floating away from the thin plate you can see. It now lies flat against the machine.
- The Spirit Codex's Wireless cargo page is rewritten for aiming and filters.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
