Tribal Power is shamanic technomancy for Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge.

Energy is Spirit Pulse — rhythmic beats, not furnace FE heat. Processing flows through a Totem Lattice: Resonance Totems linked by song, chalk, and proximity. Ores walk Echo Stages on the Song Bench instead of vanishing into a magic smelter.

Rewritten for modern NeoForge and featured in the Ninjacat Skies modpack — also fully playable standalone.

Requirements

  • Minecraft: 1.21.1
  • Loader: NeoForge 21.1.249
  • Java: 21

Core systems

  • Spirit Pulse (SP) — Drumheart, Ley Collector, Echo-catalyzed Pulse Resonator
  • Totem Lattice — Resonance Totems, Ritual Chalk, Lattice Conductor routing
  • Echo Stages — Song Bench: Shatter → Attune → Bind → Manifest
  • Ancestral / Deep Cache — local storage and March-linked bulk vault
  • Seals & Rites — Rite Pedestal boons amplified by Pulse Cells
  • Spiritgear — Pulse-fed tools
  • Gate Drum — portal into The March (20 Pulse)

Getting started

  1. Craft or take a Spirit Codex (creative tab: Tribal Power).
  2. Place a Drumheart or seat a reusable Echo catalyst in a Pulse Resonator beside two different totems.
  3. Plant Resonance Totems near a Song Bench; seal links with Ritual Chalk.
  4. Seat raw ore on the bench and start the song.
  5. Charge and strike a Gate Drum to enter The March.

The March

A shamanic otherworld with noise hills, multi-biome layout (Steppe, Highlands, Crystal Fields), March flora, ores, ruin pillars, and Spirit Wisp / March Walker spawns.

The Returning Song

Animated night auroras, a distinct March sky, three new breedable animals and ten hostile spirits. Brush gentle fauna for renewable materials and recover guardian reagents for Echo processing. Overworld forests and swamps share species with the March. Client settings control sky brightness and detail.
