# Tribal Power

**CurseForge Summary (one line):** Shamanic technomancy for NeoForge 1.21.1 — Spirit Pulse, Totem Lattice, Song Bench Echo, and The March.

Tribal Power binds spirit and machine. Energy is **Spirit Pulse** — rhythmic beats, not furnace FE heat. Processing flows through a **Totem Lattice**: Resonance Totems linked by song, chalk, and proximity. Ores walk **Echo Stages** on the **Song Bench** instead of vanishing into a magic smelter.

Rewritten for modern NeoForge and featured in the **Ninjacat Skies** modpack — also fully playable standalone.

## Core systems

- **Spirit Pulse (SP)** — native power API; Drumheart, Ley Collector, and coal-fueled Pulse Resonator
- **Totem Lattice** — Resonance Totems, Ritual Chalk links, Lattice Conductor routing
- **Echo Stages** — Song Bench refine loop: Shatter → Attune → Bind → Manifest
- **Ancestral / Deep Cache** — local storage and March-linked bulk vault
- **Seals & Rites** — Rite Pedestal spirit boons amplified by Pulse Cells
- **Spiritgear** — Pulse-fed tools
- **Gate Drum** — portal into **The March** (20 Pulse)

## Getting started

1. Craft or take a **Spirit Codex** (creative tab: Tribal Power).
2. Place a **Drumheart** or feed a **Pulse Resonator** with coal/charcoal.
3. Plant **Resonance Totems** near a **Song Bench**; seal links with **Ritual Chalk**.
4. Seat raw ore on the bench and start the song.
5. Charge and strike a **Gate Drum** to enter The March.

## The March

A shamanic otherworld with noise hills, multi-biome layout (Steppe, Highlands, Crystal Fields), March flora, ores, ruin pillars, and Spirit Wisp / March Walker spawns.
