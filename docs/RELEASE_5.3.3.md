# Tribal Power 5.3.3 - Hooks and chimes

Machines tell the truth about their Pulse, camp decor hangs where you want it, and the Wind Charm finally sounds like one.

- **Jade shows real Pulse.** Looking at a full machine no longer reads 0: the tooltip now asks the server for what the machine holds, what it makes a second and, for machines that spend Pulse, what it draws.
- **The Pulse HUD shows up for all your Pulse gear.** Holding any Spiritgear tool or armour piece, a songbook, a Pulse bow, the maul, the staff or a cell brings up the Pulse readout, not just the five items it used to know about.
- **Decor hangs on almost anything.** The Echo Sconce, Wall Shelf, Wind Charm, Woven Mat and Tribe Banners no longer need a full solid block: fences, walls, glass panes, chains, leaves, slabs and logs all hold them. They still drop when what holds them is broken.
- **A new Wind Charm.** Five copper rods of different lengths, a striker and a crystal sail on a real 3D model, with new chimes tuned to a pentatonic scale in place of the amethyst sound. It rings more in rain and a lot in storms, is quieter under a roof, and knocks a rod when you walk through it. Place it against the side of a block and it hangs from a bracket on that wall.
- **New: the Hanging Rack.** A rail with four copper hooks that sits high on whatever it is fixed to. Pots, tools and bundles hang from the hooks and sway a little. It works like the Wall Shelf, comparator and all, and you walk under it.
- **Take fittings back off your gear.** Right-click a piece in your inventory with an empty cursor to take out its seated Pulse Cell, charge and all. On a hood with goggles, the next right-click gives the goggles back as the Ley Lens they were made from.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
