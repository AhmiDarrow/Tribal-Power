# Tribal Power 3.0.0 — The Nine Tribes

- The Nine Tribes: nine camp structures in the Overworld and the March, each with a Tribe Hearth, Tribe Banner, totem and four Tribal Kin (Elder, Drummer, Hunter, Weaver). Offerings, kills and trades raise standing through Guest, Friend, Kin and Voice; Elders trade two offers per rank and press a Tribe Mark into a Voice's hand. Kinship Totems turn a Tribe Mark into an extra Resonator voice, up to fifteen in all. `/tribalpower standing` prints the nine standings.
- The March remembers: Ancestor Halls with twelve readable Lore Tablets and Loom Thread loot, the Drum Circle and its Silent Drum, the Crystal Spire waystation, and The Unsung, a three-phase drum-spirit boss (Beat, Chorus, Silence) woken by a four-beat rhythm and resynced by it. Drops an Unsung Heart, Loom Thread and Resonant Cores; advancement "The Drum Remembers".
- The sixth voice: Loom joins the five attunements. Loom Thread, Unsung Heart, Resonance Totem (Loom), the Loom Seal's Tension blessing, the Echo Unweave station that reverses the lattice and salvages worn Spiritgear, and the Sixfold Staff's Tether and Stitch. Datapack station `echo_unweave`.
- World rites: six consumable Rite Tablets used on a sealed Ritual Brazier that draw 400 to 1,200 Pulse from the lattice: Rain Calling, Sky Clearing, Dawn Calling (game rule `tribalpower:allowDawnRite`), Green Blessing, Still Night and Ley Binding, which joins two totems with a thirty-minute ley line.
- Bound spirits: Bonding Charms bond Lantern Foxes (Night Vision, ore glints, carried light), Mossbacks (nine-slot saddlebag) and Dawn Stags (rideable without a saddle). Bonded animals follow, teleport, never despawn and yield double when brushed.
- Shared camps: `/tribalpower camp` create, invite, join, leave, kick, info and rename, plus the Camp Charter item. Members share a 54-slot camp vault, count as owners of each other's devices, pool twelve Wayanchors and mirror a quarter of their tribe standing to the camp.
- Ley Lens ley sight, Pulse Gauge and Pulse Threshold redstone blocks, and Codex diagnostics: sneak-use the Spirit Codex on any Tribal block for a plain report of its power, voices and pauses.
- Art: Blender-authored Kin and Unsung rigs with painted atlases and glow maps, 3.0 block and item textures in the Living Lattice palette, nine tribe glyphs, structure and block review renders, and Codex portraits for the Kin, The Unsung, the structures and the nine tribe crests. The Codex now carries 101 teachings.
- Tooling: `tools/generate_structures.py` (nbtlib structure templates and worldgen), `tools/generate_tribe_data.py`, `tools/render_blocks.py`, `tools/render_structures.py`, `tools/verify_lang.py`; `generate_assets.py` merges lang keys instead of overwriting them; the old `--entities` painter writes are retired in favour of the Blender bestiary.
- Compatibility: content only. Existing IDs, storage slots and saves are stable; `spirit_staff` keeps its id and is renamed Sixfold Staff. New structures appear in newly generated chunks.

Validation: Java 21 / Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.249; production build and all 61 server GameTests passed (Lattice, Bestiary, Camp, Familiar, Loom, March, Rite and Tribe holders). A headless client showcase (Xvfb + llvmpipe) rendered every 3.0 block, entity, structure and screen without model, texture or render errors (`art/showcase-3.0.png`). Codex, lang and painter regeneration checks pass (`verify_codex.py`, `verify_lang.py`, `git status` clean after every `tools/art/*.py`).
