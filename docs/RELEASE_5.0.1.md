# Tribal Power 5.0.1 - Urns in their place

One fix: the four Soul Urn recipes named a recipe-book category that does not exist.

- **The Soul Urns sit with the equipment.** Their crafting recipes said `tools`, which is not a category the recipe book has. The game quietly filed them under miscellaneous, and KubeJS warned about it on every load of any pack that carries the mod. They now say `equipment`, like the rest of the camp's tools. The recipes themselves do not change.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
