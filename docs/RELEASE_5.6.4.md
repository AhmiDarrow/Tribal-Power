# Tribal Power 5.6.4 - Beans on the Ridge

Glimmer Beans finally grow wild. Nothing new to build; saves load as they are.

- **Glimmer Beans root in ridge stone.** The Glimmer Ridge is moonstone and moss agate at the surface, and the bean would only take on soil, so every wild patch failed to land and nobody ever found one. The Glimmer Bean now roots in moonstone and moss agate as readily as soil, both in the wild and under your hoe. Chunks the ridge generates from now on carry the patches; ground you have already walked stays as it is.
- **Glimmer Beans in the Crystal Fields too.** The ridge is a narrow country. Wild Glimmer Bean patches now also grow across the Crystal Fields, which are far easier to find.
- **Codex.** The March Crops entry says where the bean grows and what it roots in.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
