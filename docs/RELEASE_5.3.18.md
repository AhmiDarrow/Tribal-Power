# Tribal Power 5.3.18 - Cooler March

The Ember Wastes stop swallowing the warm half of the March.

- **Hot and dry, not just hot.** The Ember Wastes now take only the March's hot, dry ground. Hot, wet land becomes Reed Fen, and hot high ground becomes Highlands, so a long flight through a warm stretch passes through several biomes instead of one.
- **Warmer neighbours.** The Reed Fen, Highlands and Steppe now reach into warmer climates, so they also show up in the hot parts of the March.
- **Measured on a real world.** Along a 7,000-block stretch of one server's March, the Ember Wastes drop from 44% of the land to 28%. Across the whole map they drop from about 16% to 11%.
- **Existing worlds.** Land you have already explored keeps its biomes. New ground uses the new layout, so a sharp biome edge can appear where old land meets new. Nothing is regenerated or reset.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
