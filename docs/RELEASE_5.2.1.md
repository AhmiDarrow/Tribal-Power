# Tribal Power 5.2.1 - Sharper keys

Two fixes from the first day of 5.1's controls.

- **The Gear screen is readable.** Its labels were drawn before the screen's background blur and came out smeared; the panel is drawn after it now, and it is wider so no label runs under its button.
- **Key names fit the Controls list.** "Gear screen", "Ley goggles", "Staff: next voice", "Satchel vault", "Pulse HUD"; the drums keep their names.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
