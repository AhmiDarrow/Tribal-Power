- Workshop, Seal Loom, camp hands, logic plates, and Bound Effigy speak through lang keys. Old English NBT still displays as written.
- Spirit Codex chrome (title, search, buttons, spoiler confirm) is translated. Teaching bodies stay authored English.
- A Pulse Cell carries Pulse for tools, spells and travel. Held redstone pauses generators other than the Drumheart. Song Bench waits for stone or an Echo-stage item; raw metal is grit on Echo Shatter. JEI lists every Sixfold Staff cost.

- Grit shatter is 4 seconds at 20 Pulse/s before pack settings. Ley Collector diagnose uses the live beat. Stone Font click and comparator use the same work time as the tick.
