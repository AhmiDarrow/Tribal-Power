# Tribal Power 5.3.1 - Bound stones

Pulse Cairns now snap together into one carved pile you can see, and a pass over every block model closes the see-through gaps. This release carries everything from 5.3.0, which was withdrawn.

- **Touching cairns become one stone.** Set Pulse Cairns against each other on any side and the seams between them go: a pair, a wall or a 4x4x4 block reads as one bound, rune-carved mass with copper banding only round its outer edges. Each stone wears a tribe's rune, picked by where it sits, so a big pile is a patchwork of all nine.
- **The pile shows its charge.** The runes and the veins through the stone glow brighter as the pile fills, in the tribe colours, and the whole pile gives off light with it, from a faint glimmer when empty to near full light when full. Every stone in a pile shows the same level.
- **Touching cairns are one store.** Two stones hold 8,000, three 12,000, up to 64 stones and **256,000 Pulse**. Any stone reads the whole pile, fills and drains it, and gives a comparator its level. Each stone keeps its own 4,000 share inside, so a stone you break takes only its share. The old five-stone column limit is gone, and a bigger pile fills faster: 200 a second for every stone.
- **A Pulse Cell fills as far as the source holds.** One right-click on a Drumheart, a Resonator, a generator or a cairn pile tops the cell up as far as it has room, rather than 25 or 100 at a time.
- **The March crops reach AgriCraft.** AgriCraft 4.0.17 reads its crops, soils and crosses from different folders than older builds, so in a pack with the CurseForge release none of the March crops or soils were known to it. They ship in both places now and load on either.
- **No more holes in the models.** Looking into the Ley Collector's bowl or the Stone Font, or up under an Ancestral Cache, showed sky through missing inner faces. Those are closed, along with the same kind of gap on the Deep Cache, the Resonance Totems, the Hearth Pot's neck, the Guardian Altar, the Echo blocks, the Pulse Resonator, the Resonance Mesh, the Ritual Brazier, the Seal Loom, the Song Bench, the Songkeeper Drum and the Spirit Cistern.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
