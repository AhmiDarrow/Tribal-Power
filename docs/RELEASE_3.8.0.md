# Tribal Power 3.8.0 - Measure twice

Something to build against, and four readouts that were quietly lying to you.

- **Builder's Chalk.** A second kind of chalk, never spent, that stands a **hologram** of a
  shape in the world: a ghost block in every space the shape still needs. Use it on a block
  to set the layout down and it keeps standing while the chalk sits anywhere in your
  **hotbar**, so you put it away, take up blocks and build inside the ghosts. Each one goes
  out as you fill it, so what is left glowing is exactly what is left to build. Nine shapes
  - square, circle, cross, hexagon, walls, dome, hut, longhouse and **lattice camp** - from
  two blocks out to **128**, which is a circle 257 across. Sneak and right-click to grow,
  sneak and left-click to shrink; sneaking with the chalk out never breaks a block. It is a
  stick of Ritual Chalk and a Spirit Shard.
- **The Conductor could not see a Pulse Cairn.** A Cairn swallows 200 Pulse a second out of
  the same generators a Lattice Conductor draws on, and the Conductor could only read
  generators - so a camp with a Cairn in it starved the lattice, and nothing said where the
  Pulse had gone. The Conductor now lifts a Cairn's held beat onto the totems. A station's
  own buffer is still off-limits: Pulse a machine has claimed is not the lattice's to take
  back.
- **"No Pulse" was being said to full camps.** A Conductor with every totem holding its 250
  pushed nothing, and reported the same thing it reports when there is no generator at all
  - so the Codex told you to go and fetch one while you stood next to three. It tells the
  two apart now. A full lattice is not a fault, and it starts again the moment a station
  spends some.
- **A charged lattice no longer churns.** The Conductor was drawing Pulse it could only hand
  straight back, which cost it a full refund sweep every beat once a camp was charged. It
  checks for room before it draws.
- **The Ley Lens reads machine by machine.** Pulse sight lists what each machine near you
  holds and what it is making a second, not just one total for the whole zone.
- **The Lens was drawing the wrong zone.** It drew a ring, but every Pulse scan measures
  each direction on its own, so a machine's reach is a **cube**. The corners that read as
  out of range were in it, and the eight blocks of reach above and below were not drawn at
  all. It traces the real square footprint now, and marks the height.

Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.249.
